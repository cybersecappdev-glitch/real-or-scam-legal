2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Real or Scam?. It applies to the application and its free and Premium content.

3. Data Handled by the App

The current version is designed to minimize data collection. The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • selected language;
  • game progress, scores, streaks, and completed tests;
  • identifiers of questions already shown;
  • settings such as vibration feedback;
  • a cached Premium-entitlement status, product identifier, purchase token, renewal indicator, and the time of the latest entitlement check.

This locally stored information is used to operate the game, preserve progress, and control access to Premium content.

4. Premium Subscriptions and Payments

Premium subscriptions are offered through Google Play Billing. Google processes the purchase and payment information. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or Google account password.

The app may receive purchase-related information from Google Play, such as the subscription product identifier, purchase token, and whether the purchase is currently recognized as active. This information is used to provide, restore, and verify Premium access.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Advertising and Developer Analytics

The current version does not include advertising and does not include a developer-operated analytics service. Google Play and the operating system may independently process technical, diagnostic, purchase, or security information under their own policies.

If analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud synchronization, or additional third-party services are added later, this Policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

6. Children and Family Use

The app contains child-friendly educational content. A parent or legal guardian should supervise younger users and approve purchases where required. We do not ask users to create an account or intentionally request a child's name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or messages.

Parents and guardians may remove locally stored progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Purchase records are managed through Google Play.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local progress and settings remain on the device until they are reset, cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Cached subscription information is refreshed through Google Play and may be replaced when the entitlement changes.

We cannot delete payment or transaction records held by Google. Those records are controlled by Google and the user's Google account settings.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. However, no device, software, storage method, or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Processing

Google and other operating-system providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence under their own terms and privacy policies.
